# Rosary

A digital companion for praying the Catholic Rosary. This app guides you through the prayers and mysteries, using timers and haptic feedback so you can focus on your prayer without constantly checking the screen.

## Features

- **Complete Mystery Sets**: Includes all four sets of mysteries: Joyful, Sorrowful, Glorious, and Luminous.
- **Timed Guidance**: Built-in timers for each prayer (Creed, Our Father, Hail Mary, Glory Be, etc.) to help maintain a steady pace.
- **Haptic Alerts**: 
  - A single buzz notifies you when to move to the next prayer.
  - A triple buzz signals the completion of a decade (10th Hail Mary).
- **Pause & Resume**: Easily pause the timer if you are interrupted.

## How to Use

### 1. Selecting the Mystery
On the home screen, **swipe left or right** to cycle through the four sets of mysteries (Joyful, Sorrowful, Glorious, or Luminous). Once the correct set is displayed, **tap the screen** to begin.

### 2. During Prayer
- The screen will show the current prayer and a countdown timer.
- When the timer reaches zero, the watch will **buzz**, and it will automatically advance to the next prayer.
- **Tap the screen** at any time to pause or resume the timer.

### 3. Resetting
If you need to stop and start over, **swipe up** while the prayer is active to return to the home screen.

## Technical Details
- **Supports**: Bangle.js 2
- **UI Mode**: Custom
- **Feedback**: Vibration (Haptics)
